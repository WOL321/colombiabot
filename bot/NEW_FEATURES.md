## 🎮 New Commands

### `!fact` (aliases: `!dato`, `!sabias`)
Shares a random Colombian fact in a beautiful embed.

**Example facts:**
- 🦜 Colombia has more bird species than any other country — over 1,900!
- 🌺 Colombia produces 70% of the world's emeralds.
- ☕ Colombian coffee is considered the best in the world.
- 📚 Gabriel García Márquez won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1982.

---

### `!quote` (aliases: `!frase`, `!inspiracion`)
Shares an inspirational quote from famous Colombians or Colombian proverbs.

**Example quotes:**
- 💭 "Life is not what one lived, but what one remembers." — Gabriel García Márquez
- 💭 "Music is the universal language that connects us all." — Juanes
- 💭 "El que mucho abarca, poco aprieta." (Colombian Proverb)

---

### `!flag` (alias: `!bandera`)
Shows the Colombian flag with an image and explains the meaning of each color:
- 💛 **Yellow**: Sovereignty, justice, and the rich soil
- 💙 **Blue**: The sky, the seas, loyalty, and perseverance
- ❤️ **Red**: The blood of heroes who fought for freedom

---

### `!color` (alias: `!colour`)
Shows a random flag color with its meaning and hex code.

**Example:**
```
💛 Yellow
Sovereignty, justice, and the rich Colombian soil
Hex Code: #FCD116
```

---

### `!emoji` (alias: `!emojis`)
Shows a random set of Colombian-themed emojis.

**Example sets:**
- 🇨🇴 💛 💙 ❤️
- ☕ 🌱 🏔️ 🌺
- 🦜 🦋 🐆 🌳
- 💃 🕺 🎵 🎺

---

### `!message` (aliases: `!msg`, `!random`)
Shows a random Colombian pride message with emoji variations.

**Example:**
```
🇨🇴✨ ¡Viva Colombia! ✨🇨🇴
```

## 🔄 How It Works

The bot runs on GitHub Actions:
- **Frequency:** Every 50 minutes
- **Online time:** 2 minutes per run
- **Commands:** Work during the 2-minute window
- **Messages:** Automatically sent with 80% probability
- **Reactions:** 3-5 random emojis added to each message
- **Holiday detection:** Special messages on Colombian holidays
